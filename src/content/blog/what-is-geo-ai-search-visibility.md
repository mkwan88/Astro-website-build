A growing share of searches now start in a chat window, not a search bar. Someone asks ChatGPT or
Gemini "who's a good electrician near me" instead of typing it into Google. If your business isn't
structured in a way those systems can understand and trust, you simply don't exist in that
conversation — no matter how good your Google ranking is.

## GEO builds on SEO, it doesn't replace it

Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) shares a foundation with traditional SEO: clear, well-organised
content and credible signals still matter. What changes is the emphasis. AI systems favour content
that answers a specific question directly and completely, and they lean heavily on structured data
(schema markup) to understand exactly what a business offers and where.

## What actually helps

**Direct, quotable answers.** FAQ sections that answer real customer questions in plain, complete
sentences are exactly the kind of content AI systems pull from when generating a recommendation.

**Structured data.** Schema markup for your business, services and location gives AI systems a
machine-readable summary of who you are, rather than making them infer it from unstructured prose.

**Consistency.** The same business details — name, service area, offerings — need to be
consistent everywhere they appear, since AI systems cross-reference multiple sources when forming
an answer.

## What doesn't help (yet)

There's no reliable way to "trick" an AI assistant into recommending a business it wouldn't
otherwise recommend, and be wary of anyone promising guaranteed placement in AI answers — no one
controls that outcome. The honest version of GEO is putting your business in the strongest possible
position to be understood and surfaced accurately, not gaming a black box.
