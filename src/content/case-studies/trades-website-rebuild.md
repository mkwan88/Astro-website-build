## The challenge

The business had a website built roughly a decade earlier that hadn't been touched since. It loaded
slowly, broke on mobile, and had never been structured around the specific services or suburbs the
business actually covered — everything sat on a single generic page.

## The approach

We rebuilt the site from scratch with dedicated pages for each core service and suburb combination,
optimised for speed and mobile from the ground up, and included clear calls to action matched to
how tradie enquiries actually happen — a fast call or message, not a long contact form.

## The results

Within weeks of launch, the new structure began ranking for suburb-specific searches the old
single-page site had never targeted, reaching the #1 position for six of its core suburb-service
combinations within the following quarter.
