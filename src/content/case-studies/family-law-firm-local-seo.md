## The challenge

A well-regarded but under-marketed family law firm was sitting on page two of Google for every
practice area that mattered — family law, divorce, and custody — despite handling more cases
locally than several competitors ranking above them. Their Google Business Profile was claimed but
essentially untouched: no categories set beyond the default, no posts, and only a handful of photos.

## The approach

We rebuilt the site's service-page structure around the specific searches prospective clients were
actually using, rewrote every page's content and metadata, and did a full rebuild of the Google
Business Profile — correct categories, service descriptions, regular posting, and a review
response cadence the firm could sustain on their own.

## The results

Within five months, the firm moved from page two to the top three map-pack results for its three
core practice areas, and organic enquiries through the website grew 184% over the same period.
